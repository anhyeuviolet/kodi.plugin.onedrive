<objective>
Prove the protocol works through this add-on's own code, against the live provider, with a real
account — before the television is involved.

Purpose: everything up to here is proven against a fake endpoint. That is the right way to test the
logic and it says nothing about whether this registration, this scope set and this authority still
behave the way the spike measured them. Doing this off the television first is deliberate: if
sign-in fails here it is a protocol problem, and if it fails on the television after passing here it
is a Kodi problem. Merging the two into one session gives a failure that could be either.

Two measurements close gaps the design was built over. Whether the identity token carries a name
claim for this account decides whether the label path or its fallback is the one that runs, and the
fallback's own field was never transcribed from the spike output. And whichever failure codes appear
are the only ones this project will ever observe from a tenant that permits the grant.

Output: a recorded live pass, and a runbook that says what was seen and what still cannot be.
</objective>
